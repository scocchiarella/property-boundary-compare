Roofs and roads come live from OpenStreetMap (Overpass) — structures on the lot itself are excluded, distances are from the house. Woods/open is estimated from the satellite tiles (texture analysis; winter imagery reads conservative, real leaf-on canopy is a bit higher). Road noise is a rough class+distance model, not a measurement. Beds/baths/sqft are manual: grab them from the listing; they save in this browser per property name.
